01 — About

About

Hi, I’m Hongyu Guo — an indie developer. I design and build small software that makes digital life safer, calmer, and a little more beautiful: a password manager you can trust, wallpapers that make your Mac feel alive.

Right now I’m focused on SafeVault and MaiKer, both designed, built, and shipped by myself across five platforms.

02 — Projects

Projects

Password manager

SafeVault

Close the lid at night — your passwords rest in the cloud as ciphertext. Built on the AES-256-GCM standard with authenticated encryption and PBKDF2 key derivation, plaintext never leaves your device; sync transmits ciphertext only, so a breached server can recover not a single password.
